# Research References: African Precipitation Extremes, Aerosols, Dust, and EarthCARE

This document lists the 20 research articles provided, with their
publication year and a short description of the main finding or research

## 1. Precipitation extremes and climate warming

### 1.1 Robust Intensification of Projected Regional Precipitation Extremes over Africa

-   **Year:** 2026
-   **Short description:** Uses climate simulations to project changes
    in extreme rainfall across Africa. It finds that annual maximum
    one-day precipitation is expected to intensify in many regions by
    the late 21st century, with warming and atmospheric heating
    contributing to the increase.

### 1.2 Regionally High Risk Increase for Precipitation Extreme Events under Global Warming

-   **Year:** 2023
-   **Short description:** Examines how global warming may increase the
    frequency of extreme precipitation events. It highlights the role of
    regional atmospheric dynamics, which can amplify the increase in
    rainfall extremes beyond the effect of warming alone.

### 1.3 Super-Clausius--Clapeyron Scaling of Extreme Precipitation Explained by Shift from Stratiform to Convective Rain Type

-   **Year:** 2025
-   **Short description:** Investigates why extreme rainfall can
    increase faster than the rate expected from the basic
    Clausius--Clapeyron relationship. It attributes this enhanced
    scaling mainly to a shift from widespread stratiform rain toward
    more intense convective rain.

### 1.4 Scaling of Rainfall Intensity and Frequency with Rising Temperatures

-   **Year:** 2026
-   **Short description:** Studies how rainfall intensity and frequency
    respond to rising temperatures. It describes a possible trade-off in
    which rainfall events become more intense while their frequency
    changes, with extreme rainfall showing strong sensitivity to
    warming.

### 1.5 Thermodynamic Versus Large-Scale Controls on Extreme Precipitation: Temporal Scale Dependence and Clausius--Clapeyron Scaling Redefined

-   **Year:** 2025
-   **Short description:** Separates the influence of atmospheric
    moisture and temperature (thermodynamic controls) from large-scale
    circulation. It finds that thermodynamics are especially important
    for hourly precipitation extremes, while circulation plays a
    stronger role at daily timescales.

### 1.6 Dependence of Convective Precipitation Extremes on Near-Surface Relative Humidity

-   **Year:** 2025
-   **Short description:** Examines how near-surface relative humidity
    affects extreme convective rainfall. It emphasizes that relative
    humidity should be considered separately from specific humidity when
    estimating how precipitation extremes may change over land.

## 2. Atmospheric dust and aerosols in Africa

### 2.1 A Global Decline in Atmospheric Dust during the 21st Century

-   **Year:** 2026
-   **Short description:** Investigates changes in atmospheric dust over
    the 21st century and reports a decline in global dust levels,
    including in North Africa and Asia. It also discusses how reduced
    dust can influence the Earth's energy balance and regional warming.

### 2.2 Trend of North African Dust Storms and Potential Link to Climate Change

-   **Year:** 2026
-   **Short description:** Examines long-term changes in North African
    dust-storm activity and its possible climate drivers. The study
    reports a decline in dust storms over recent decades and discusses
    links with ocean-atmosphere variability, vegetation, and wind
    patterns.

### 2.3 Variation, Distribution and Trends of Aerosol Optical Properties in Africa during 2000--2022

-   **Year:** 2025
-   **Short description:** Analyses how aerosol optical properties vary
    across Africa over 2000--2022. It reports different patterns across
    regions, including decreases in some arid areas and increases in
    parts of the Sahel and Central Africa, and investigates
    relationships with air temperature.

### 2.4 Twenty Years of Satellite Observations Reveal a Shift in the Diurnal Trend of Dust Optical Depth over West Africa

-   **Year:** 2026
-   **Short description:** Uses two decades of satellite observations to
    study how dust optical depth changes throughout the day over West
    Africa. It reports a shift in the daily pattern, with potential
    implications for air quality, radiative forcing, and interactions
    between aerosols and clouds.

### 2.5 Seasonal AOD Analysis Based on AERONET Observations in North and West Africa over 2010--2019

-   **Year:** 2025
-   **Short description:** Uses ground-based AERONET observations to
    examine seasonal aerosol optical depth (AOD) and aerosol types in
    North and West Africa. It focuses on the importance of Saharan dust
    and seasonal changes associated with the monsoon.

### 2.6 Spatiotemporal Analysis of Atmospheric Aerosols in African Environments Using MERRA-2 Data (1980--2024): Impacts on Climate Extremes

-   **Year:** 2025
-   **Short description:** Uses the MERRA-2 atmospheric reanalysis to
    investigate long-term aerosol patterns across Africa and their
    relationship with climate extremes. It explores possible links
    between aerosols, temperature changes, heatwaves, and precipitation
    variability.

### 2.7 Climatology and Dynamics of Aerosol Optical Properties from Selected AERONET Sites over West Africa

-   **Year:** 2025
-   **Short description:** Studies the seasonal behavior and atmospheric
    characteristics of aerosols using selected AERONET stations in West
    Africa. It compares locations and discusses the influence of Saharan
    dust and maritime air on aerosol optical properties.

## 3. EarthCARE mission and atmospheric observations

### 3.1 The EarthCARE Mission -- Science and System Overview

-   **Year:** 2023
-   **Short description:** Introduces the EarthCARE satellite mission
    and its scientific objectives. It explains how the mission observes
    aerosols, clouds, precipitation, and radiation to improve
    understanding of atmospheric processes and Earth's energy balance.

### 3.2 The EarthCARE Mission: Science Data Processing Chain Overview

-   **Year:** 2024
-   **Short description:** Describes how measurements from EarthCARE
    instruments are processed into scientific data products. It explains
    the processing chain and how the resulting observations can support
    weather forecasting and climate research.

### 3.3 First Insights into Deep Convection by the Doppler Velocity Measurements of the EarthCARE Cloud Profiling Radar

-   **Year:** 2025
-   **Short description:** Explores how Doppler velocity measurements
    from the EarthCARE Cloud Profiling Radar reveal vertical air and
    particle motion inside deep convective clouds. These observations
    help researchers investigate storm dynamics and cloud development.

### 3.4 The Classification of Atmospheric Hydrometeors and Aerosols from the EarthCARE Radar and Lidar: The A-TC, C-TC and AC-TC Products

-   **Year:** 2023
-   **Short description:** Explains how EarthCARE radar and lidar
    observations are combined to classify atmospheric targets such as
    clouds, precipitation particles, and aerosols. The combined
    measurements help improve the identification and characterization of
    atmospheric layers.

### 3.5 A Unified Synergistic Retrieval of Clouds, Aerosols, and Precipitation from EarthCARE: The ACM-CAP Product

-   **Year:** 2023
-   **Short description:** Presents the EarthCARE ACM-CAP data product,
    which combines observations to retrieve information about clouds,
    aerosols, and precipitation. It aims to provide a more unified view
    of atmospheric conditions, including ice clouds, snow, and
    light-to-moderate rain.

### 3.6 Exploring Vertical Motions in Convective and Stratiform Precipitation Using Spaceborne Radar Observations: Insights from EarthCARE and GPM Coincidence Dataset

-   **Year:** 2026
-   **Short description:** Uses coincident observations from EarthCARE
    and the Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) mission to
    investigate vertical motions in different types of precipitation.
    The study helps reveal the physical processes inside deep clouds and
    supports improvements in precipitation retrieval and classification.

### 3.7 Cloud Top Heights and Aerosol Layer Properties from EarthCARE Lidar Observations: The A-CTH and A-ALD Products

-   **Year:** 2023
-   **Short description:** Describes EarthCARE lidar products for
    estimating cloud-top height and aerosol-layer properties. These
    measurements help researchers understand the vertical structure of
    clouds and aerosols and how they interact in the atmosphere.

## Relevance to an African climate-trend investigation

Together, these papers provide background for investigating how
precipitation extremes, atmospheric moisture, dust, and aerosols vary
across Africa. The precipitation studies help explain possible changes
in rainfall intensity and frequency; the aerosol studies provide context
for dust and atmospheric composition; and the EarthCARE papers describe
satellite observations that can reveal the vertical structure of clouds,
aerosols, and precipitation.
