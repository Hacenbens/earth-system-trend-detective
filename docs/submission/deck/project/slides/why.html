The Sahel lives on four months of rain. Rain-fed agriculture and pastoralism depend on both how much rain falls and how evenly it falls: one long dry spell in August can ruin a harvest. Rainfall drops steeply from the wet south-west to the dry north, so a single Sahel average hides very different places.
