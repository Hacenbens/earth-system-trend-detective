NASA MERRA-2 reanalysis + NASA GPM IMERG V07, 1998–2025 · Associations, not proof of cause · Analysis code drafted with AI assistance (Claude Code)

For each candidate cause we wrote down what it predicts and checked it in NASA’s MERRA-2 reanalysis. One mechanism passes every test: the monsoon. Wet seasons bring stronger westerly inflow of Atlantic moisture along the southern Sahel, more convergence and more rising air. Moisture alone explains about half of the year-to-year rainfall, even on years the model has never seen. Over 1998–2025 water vapour rose about 4 percent per decade. The alternatives fail: the rainy season did not warm (the east even cooled, because wetter land evaporates more), wind shear did not increase, and dust shows no link with rainfall. These are associations in a reanalysis, not proof of cause.
