ESA–JAXA EarthCARE radar (CPR) + lidar (ATLID), Level 2 · 4 case studies, 2024–2025: processes, not trends · Analysis code drafted with AI assistance (Claude Code)

Trends tell us what changed; EarthCARE shows how the rain is made today. Its radar and lidar slice through the atmosphere. Here, over the eastern Sahel on 2 September 2024, a deep storm reaches the tropopause, heavy rain falls below the melting level at 4.7 kilometres, and the updrafts reach 6 to 9 metres per second. The Saharan dust layer, between 1 and 6 kilometres, lies next to the storm, not inside it, consistent with what 28 years of data say: dust is not what makes seasons wet or dry. EarthCARE started in 2024, so we use it to illustrate processes, never as a trend.
